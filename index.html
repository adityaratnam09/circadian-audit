Your chronotype wheel

Think of the chronotype wheel as a 24-hour clock face, midnight at the top, noon at the bottom, running clockwise. The further the shaded region bulges out towards the rim at a given hour, the sharper your performance was around that time. A dent in towards the centre indicates a slump in performance. The scale auto-adjusts to your best and worst hours, and updates automatically as you log more results. Solid dots mark the hours you have actually logged your performance; the rest is smoothly interpolated between them.

About this project

Created by Aditya Rajiv Ratnam, the Circadian Rhythm Audit is a small self-contained tool for tracking your own natural cognitive rhythm. Run a short reaction, math or memory test a few times a day and this page charts how your performance rises and falls across the 24-hour clock, so you can see when your brain is sharpest and plan study or exercise around it.
